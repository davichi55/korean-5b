2문법 · Grammar 15 points · ★ = on the 5B 문법 handout

Read each explanation once, then build sentences with it. The ★ points are the ones the teacher practised most.

1과
  • -(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 ★ almost happened
  • -아/어서 그런지 ★ maybe because ~
  • (-(으)면) -(으)ㄹ수록 ★ the more ~, the more ~
  • -기보다(는) rather than ~ · -(으)ㄴ/는 이상 since / as long as ~
2과
  • -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 ★ there's no way that ~
  • -는 바람에 ★ because something suddenly happened (bad result)
  • -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 ★ to pretend to ~
  • -는 둥 마는 둥 half-heartedly · -답니다 storyteller ending
3과
  • -(으)ㄹ걸(요) ★ I think ~ / probably ~ (soft guess)
  • -다고 (해서) -는 것은 아니다 ★ just because ~ doesn't mean ~
  • -아/어 두다 ★ do in advance and keep
  • -(느)냐에 따라 depending on ~ · -아/어 내다 manage to ~ in the end
4과
  • -(으)ㄹ 정도로 ★ so ~ that ~
  • -기만 하다 ★ only does ~
  • 뭔가 / 누군가 / 어딘가 / 언젠가 ★ something / someone / somewhere / some day
  • -(으)ㅁ으로써 by doing ~ (written)
